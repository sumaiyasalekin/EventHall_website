Workflow C

Deposit to confirmed reservation

The backend is authoritative—not the browser return page.

1

Branch on deposit requirement

If required, create hosted checkout inside a valid hold. If not required, continue with staff approval and terms validation.

2

Verify the payment event

Validate the signed webhook, amount, currency, reference, replay protection, and duplicate event ID.

3

Confirm atomically

Revalidate availability, approval, accepted terms, and deposit condition in one transaction.

4

Deliver or recover

On success, send reference, schedule, receipt, balance date, next steps, and calendar file. On conflict or expired hold, do not double-book; route payment exceptions to staff.

Clarity before commitment

A few important details.

The experience is clear to customers and safe for staff.

Does an inquiry reserve the date?

No. It creates a request reference. A quote also does not block a date unless staff explicitly creates a timed hold.

When is the reservation confirmed?

After staff approval, exact quote and terms acceptance, required deposit condition, and atomic server-side validation.

What if payment arrives after a hold expires?

The system does not double-book. It routes the payment to staff for the agreed refund or alternative-date process.

Can customers use the website without the app?

Yes. Public discovery, inquiry, and the customer web portal remain available in the browser.
